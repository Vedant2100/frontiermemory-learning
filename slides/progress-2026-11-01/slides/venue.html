FORGE Data and Benchmarking explicitly welcomes "benchmark audits revealing saturation, contamination, construct-validity problems" and replications; it is a conference track, double-anonymous, IEEE format, 4 pages plus 1 of references, due Nov 15 AoE. AGENT, AGENTVERIFY and LLM4Code at ICSE all publish in IEEE proceedings, so the same work cannot also go to FORGE; FORGE also gives two extra days. The ICSE Industry Challenge topic "Agent Memory in the Wild" asks for a new memory system evaluated on an established long-term memory benchmark and an agent workload, against no memory, full-history replay and RAG over transcripts, with cost, latency and storage, 5 to 10 pages; we have the no-memory comparison and the agent workload but not the rest, and it is due two days before FORGE. MSR Registered Reports (Nov 13/20) review a study plan before data collection, so they fit a future M5 study, not this one. AAMAS workshops (calls expected about Jan '27) fit only a multi-agent framing.
